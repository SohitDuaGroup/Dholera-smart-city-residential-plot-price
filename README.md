3. Factors Influencing the Dholera Smart City Residential Plot Price
A common mistake investors make is assuming all land in Dholera holds equal value. Several distinct factors dictate why the Dholera smart city residential plot price fluctuates between pre-launch offers and fully registered plots.

A. Proximity to the Activation Area (TP 2)
The Activation Area spans 22.5 square kilometers within Town Planning Scheme 2. It serves as the launchpad of the entire smart city. Roads, water lines, sewage systems, and power stations are fully functional here. Naturally, residential plots located inside or immediately adjacent to TP 2 command a premium, causing the baseline Dholera smart city residential plot price to rise relative to outlying zones.

B. Road Width and Plot Positioning
Dholera’s master plan integrates massive arterial roads ranging from 55 meters to 250 meters in width. Within residential societies, internal roads are usually 12 to 18 meters wide.

Corner Plots and Main Road Facing Plots: Command a Preferential Location Charge (PLC). In major modern townships, a standard PLC of 10% is levied on Corner & 12-Meter Road facing plots above the base Dholera smart city residential plot price.
Internal Plots: Offer quieter residential living environments and standard pricing.
C. Amenities and Developer History
Cheap but very risky is a raw piece of agricultural land sitting there for conversion. On the other hand, a project within an integrated township offers gated security, clubhouses, landscaped gardens and paved internal roads. The value additions justify higher core Basic Sale Price (BSP) while calculating the total Dholera smart city residential plot price.

D. Approvals under NA (Non-Agricultural) and RERA
In Gujarat, for residential use, land has to get NA (Non-Agricultural) certification. Also, the projects have to be registered with Gujarat Real Estate Regulatory Authority (GUJRERA) Dholera smart city residential plot price changes are directly related to the evolution of these approvals.

Pre-Launch Phase: Lower entry pricing (e.g., ₹7,800/Sq. Yd.) before final paperwork.
Launch/Execution Phase: Once NA, NOC, and Plan Pass are secured, the Dholera smart city residential plot price automatically corrects upward (e.g., ₹8,500/Sq. Yd.), and the Buyer-Builder Agreement (BBA) executes.
